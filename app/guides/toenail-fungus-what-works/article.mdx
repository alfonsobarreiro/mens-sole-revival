# Toenail Fungus: What Works, What's a Scam, and Where to Start

Toenail fungus isn’t dangerous.  
It’s just stubborn.

Most advice promises fast fixes or miracle products, then delivers results that don’t line up with reality.

This page is the short version and the map. It ranks the five ways to treat toenail fungus by how often they produce a normal nail, names the ones that are a year lost, and points to the five guides that cover each step in depth: whether it's fungus at all, the treatments compared, the twelve months of treatment, keeping it from coming back, and what the home remedies have behind them.

## My Experience With Toenail Fungus

I dealt with toenail fungus myself.

No miracle product cleared it, and there was no turning point I could point to.

What worked was showing up: filing the nail, applying the treatment, keeping the shoes dry. Every day for months, even when nothing looked different.

## What works, ranked

The five routes, from the one that cures the most to the one that cures the least. The figures are complete cure, a nail fully normal to look at, from the trials that approved each treatment. The detail and the sources are in [treatments, compared](/guides/toenail-fungus-treatments-compared).

1. **Oral terbinafine.** A daily tablet for 12 weeks. About 4 men in 10 reach a completely normal nail and about 7 in 10 are fungus-free on the lab test. A cheap generic, with a liver blood test first and your medication list checked. The best odds by a distance.
2. **Oral itraconazole.** Roughly 1 man in 7 reaches a normal nail. The pill for men who can't take terbinafine, or whose lab test finds a yeast or mold. It interacts with several common drugs, including some statins.
3. **Prescription topicals.** Efinaconazole (Jublia), tavaborole (Kerydin), and ciclopirox (Penlac), painted on daily for 48 weeks. Between about 1 in 6 and 1 in 18 reach a normal nail. For mild infections at the tip, and for men who can't take the pill.
4. **Nail thinning.** Not a cure on its own. It makes every other route work better and costs an office visit or a tube of 40% urea cream.
5. **Laser.** Cleared for a "temporary increase in clear nail", which is a cosmetic claim. Small, inconsistent studies; several hundred to over a thousand dollars; almost never covered.

Pill plus topical does better than either alone in stubborn cases, and pill plus thinning is standard practice.

## What's a scam, or close to it

**Anything promising results in days or weeks.** A toenail grows about a millimeter a month. New, clean nail starts at the cuticle and takes a year to reach the tip. The damaged nail in front of it never repairs; it grows out and gets trimmed off. No product changes that calendar.

**Athlete's foot cream on the nail.** Terbinafine cream, Lamisil AT and the rest, is the right thing for the peeling between your toes and the wrong thing for the nail, because it doesn't get through the plate. Treat the skin with it. Treat the nail with something that reaches the nail.

**Drugstore "fungal nail" kits.** Mostly urea, which softens the nail, or a whitener. Useful for filing, useless as a cure.

**Vinegar, baking soda, garlic, mouthwash, peroxide.** No nail evidence at all. Vicks has one small uncontrolled study; tea tree oil has old, weak evidence and a rash risk. [Home remedies and laser](/guides/toenail-fungus-home-remedies-and-laser) has the trial sizes.

## The system around the nail

Whatever the treatment, three things run alongside it or the nail comes back. Treat the skin between the toes, which is where the fungus waits between nails; the [athlete's foot guide](/guides/athletes-foot-and-foot-odor-what-works) has the four-week protocol. Rotate two pairs of shoes, spray or powder each, and change wicking socks daily. Dry between the toes after every shower. The [nightly routine](/routines/daily) builds the last one in, and the [prevention guide](/guides/keeping-toenail-fungus-from-coming-back) has the rest.

## Bottom line

Toenail fungus is hard to beat because the treatment is **slow**, not because it’s complicated. Get the clipping test, take the route with the best odds, treat every day for the year, judge progress at the base of the nail rather than the tip, and keep the shoes and the skin in the system afterward. Start with [is it toenail fungus?](/guides/is-it-toenail-fungus) if the nail hasn't been tested, and [treatments, compared](/guides/toenail-fungus-treatments-compared) if it has.
